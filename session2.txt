## What was wrong

Tuleap declares a **different `limit` maximum per REST route** — 50 on `/projects`, higher on most others — and answers `400` instead of clamping the value. Our default `request.per_page = 100` therefore blew up on the very first page of the project picker.

## The fix

Rather than hardcoding 50 everywhere (which would then be wrong on routes that allow more), `lua/tuleap/api/client.lua` now keeps a per-route cap registry, learnt three ways:

1. **Seeded** with `/projects = 50`, so your first `:Tuleap projects` doesn't need to fail to find out.
2. **From the `X-PAGINATION-LIMIT-MAX` response header** on any successful request.
3. **From Tuleap's own error message** — `paginate` parses `Maximum allowed value is N`, records it, and *replays the same page* at the reduced size.

Caps are keyed on the route shape (`/projects/#/trackers`), so one tracker teaching the cap benefits every other. The retry can't loop: `per_page` strictly decreases.

Two follow-on changes:

- A successful page that advertises a smaller `limit_max` shrinks the window for subsequent pages too — otherwise `paginate` would see a short page and stop early, silently truncating your results.
- `artifacts.links` went through `client.get` with a hardcoded `limit = 100`, bypassing all of this. It now uses `paginate`, so linked artifacts get the same handling and full pagination.
